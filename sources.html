County jails & sheriff data

Jackson County, IN — Sheriff inmate roster & 48-hour release page
Daily roster + booking/release counts. Aggregate by year for Schema 2 jail columns.
 Scott County, IN — Jail page & 2022 jail study
85% substance-use prevalence; 58% mental-health prevalence; recidivism context. Fills Schema 6 prevalence columns and serves as proxy anchor for surrounding rural jails.

Where IDOC reports jail-of-admission data alongside facility data.

Each southern-Indiana county sheriff has a similar inmate-roster URL pattern (e.g., jenningscountysheriffin.gov, browncountysheriffin.org). Replicate the Jackson approach across all counties.
