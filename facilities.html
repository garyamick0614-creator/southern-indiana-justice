Recurring incident patterns 2020–2025 (news-tagged)

Cell fires — Indiana State Prison

2023–2025 saw an investigative-news-confirmed cluster of 20+ cell fires; multiple inmates with severe burns, several deaths. Triggered Gov. Braun calls for action and IDOC fire-safety reforms. Active concern

Drug trafficking — Westville & others

Repeated meth, THC, marijuana, and cell-phone smuggling cases across IDOC facilities; charge codes "trafficking with an inmate." 2025 case: 19-year-old caught smuggling at Westville. Recurring

Post-intake overdose & suicide

National pattern: >1/3 of opioid-related deaths and >1/2 of other drug deaths occur within 24 hours of incarceration. Southern Indiana mirror: high-risk window after booking, especially with fentanyl-contaminated supply. Critical window

IRACS scaling — Scott Co.

Integrated Reentry and Correctional Support pilot active at Scott County Jail since ~2022. Peer recovery coaches + reentry navigators + post-release continuity. State-funded via Indiana Division of Mental Health and Addiction. Promising

The news-headline schema

Every prison/jail incident headline can be ingested into a single shared schema for searchability and trend analysis. The shape we use:

headline_id, facility_id, facility_name, county,
year, date_published, source_name, url,
incident_type (suicide | overdose | drug_trafficking | fire | escape_attempt | abuse | riot | staff_misconduct | other),
summary_short (1-2 sentences),
source_type (TV | newspaper | county_report | press_release | social_media)
