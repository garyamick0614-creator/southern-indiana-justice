Civic context · where the decisions get made

Where these numbers get debated — and decided.

Justice-system change moves through county councils, sheriff’s offices, sheriff’s merit boards, the Indiana General Assembly, and the executive agencies (IDOC, DMHA, IDOH). SIJ tracks the civic touchpoints that affect the data on this dashboard.

Data status — backend in progress.

County council and commissioner meeting agendas are published per-county on county websites with varying formats. Aggregated meeting indexing is queued. Until that lands, this page surfaces the in-fabric resources that already track civic activity statewide.

Planned civic-life schema

county_id, county_name, year, month,
  body (council / commissioners / sheriff_merit_board / court),
  meeting_date, meeting_kind (regular / special / public_hearing),
  agenda_url, minutes_url,
  justice_related_items, jail_funding_items, reentry_items,
  public_comment_speakers, was_public_comment_open
What lands first. County council and commissioners meeting indexes for Jackson, Scott, and Jennings (the three highest-leverage focus counties) come first. After that the remaining nine focus counties roll in. Once all 12 are indexed, justice-related agenda items become a sortable filter across the dashboard.
