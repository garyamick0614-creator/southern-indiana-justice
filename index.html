How to use the side-by-side

  1. Start at Counties. Per-1,000 rates for arrests, jail population, IDOC releases, overdose deaths, 911 volume, and homelessness for Jackson and surrounding counties.
  2. Then Jails & Prisons. Facility-level patterns — suicide / overdose / drug-trafficking incident counts, plus a tagged news-headline log for every IDOC and county facility in the southern-Indiana corridor.
  3. Then Predictions. 2026–2030 projections using linear and exponential trend formulas you can audit and re-run on your own data.
  4. Always check Sources. Every number on this site links back to a primary source (IDOC report, county study, state dashboard, or named news article).

Why this exists

Coverage of incarceration, overdose, and reentry in southern Indiana is fragmented. State dashboards live on one site, county jail studies live in council-meeting PDFs, IDOC release tables live behind a navigation tree, and news incidents are scattered across a dozen TV-station websites. The result is that people with the most at stake — returning citizens, families, sheriffs, county councilors, and voters — have to assemble the picture themselves.

This site does the assembly. Every metric is sourced, every comparison is rate-normalized, and every projection is built on a published formula you can audit. It’s framework first, data second — the schemas exist whether the numbers are still being filled in or not.
