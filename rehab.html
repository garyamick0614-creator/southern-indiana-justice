Schema 5 · reentry / rehab / MAT / outreach programs

The programs interrupting the cycle.

Reentry, rehab, MAT, and outreach programs documented across the 12 focus counties. Each program belongs to one of four types: reentry (post-incarceration linkage), rehab (residential or outpatient), MAT (medication-assisted treatment), and outreach (community-level prevention).

Data status — known programs listed, per-year participation counts queued.

The major programs are documented from their own public reports. Per-county per-year participation, hours-of-service, and outcome counts depend on provider-by-provider data pulls that are queued. Until those land, this page shows the verified totals from each program’s own publications.

Statewide programs

HIRE — Hoosier Initiative for Re-Entry

Type: reentry · statewide

Job-placement program. State-funded.

1,808 placements 2024

594 partner employers · $15.84 avg wage · 15,000+ lifetime placements · 14% recidivism among completers.

IRACS — Integrated Reentry & Correctional Support

Type: reentry · Scott County Jail

State-funded via Indiana DMHA. Active since ~2022.

9.5% recidivism

Down from 26% pre-program. 7th consecutive perfect inspection March 2026. Peer recovery coaches on-site; post-release navigator continuity.

Goodwill New Beginnings + Rural Works

Type: reentry + outreach · partial coverage

Justice-involved Career Day model + rural workforce reintegration.

Strong track record

Bus-route requirement is a gatekeeper for participation; coverage in southern Indiana is partial.

Anchor county models

Jackson County — Triple Stack

Type: reentry stack · SOAR + MRT + Catholic Charities

The model SIJ uses as its outcome anchor. SOAR (benefits linkage) + Moral Reconation Therapy + Catholic Charities case management.

8% recidivism

Scott County — IRACS

Type: jail-based reentry · state pilot

See HIRE/IRACS card above. State-funded model now being studied for cross-county replication.

9.5% recidivism

SSP — Scott County Syringe Services

Type: harm reduction / outreach · HIV outbreak response, 2015

Authorized after the 2015 HIV outbreak in Austin, IN. Continues to anchor the IDOH SSP roster as one of the longest-running programs in the state.
