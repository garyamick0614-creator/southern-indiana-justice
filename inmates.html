Who is held, and where they go.

The Indiana Department of Correction publishes adult-release counts by county of admission, and individual sheriff’s offices publish jail rosters at varying intervals. This page surfaces both for the 12 focus counties.

Data status — backend in progress.

The IDOC adult-releases PDFs (2020–2025) are queued for extraction into the SIJ database. County-jail rosters require per-sheriff polling and are being added one county at a time. Until live feeds land, this page presents only the verified facts we have already published in the schema and methodology documents — no fabricated counts.

Jail substance-use & mental-health prevalence

Source: Scott County Jail Study (2022) as anchor

85% substance-use history, 58% prior mental-health diagnosis. Used as an anchor for proxy estimates in surrounding rural jails until each sheriff’s office publishes its own roster study.

Post-intake overdose risk window

Source: National jail-overdose pattern

>1/3 of opioid deaths and >1/2 of other drug deaths in jail occur in the first 24 hours after booking. Post-release weeks 1–4 are the peak overdose risk window in the community.
